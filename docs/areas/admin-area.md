# The clinic admin area (`app/admin`)

Moved out of the root `CLAUDE.md` in November 2026, word for word except where the pull request that moved it says otherwise.
A section name in quotes ("Billing state", "Roles") is a heading in one of the files under `docs/areas/`; the index at the end of the root `CLAUDE.md` says which.

## `app/admin`, the back-office surface

**`app/admin` is the back-office surface**, and its pages have fixed jobs.
Keep this map stable; do not move a job to another page or merge two of them:

| Page | Job |
|---|---|
| `/admin` | The office manager's **overview**: the notice from Pulse, what needs a look (links that stopped working, links about to expire), **Links waiting to be reactivated** (paused links a patient has asked about, each with a button to `/admin/reactivate/<id>`, the page that turns one back on after a Confirm press; see "How a link expires, pauses, and is turned back on"), a few counts (working right now, made in the last 30 days; **no play starts and no other usage numbers**, which came off on 2026-11-16 by the user's decision, matching Van's of 2026-10-08, and no words about cancelled links, which clinics cannot cancel), the newest five links (each says "Not played yet" or "Played", never how many times), and a card into each section. Not the full list of anything: it reads five counts, five rows and at most ten waiting links (`summarizeSharesForClinic()`, `listRecentSharesForClinic()` and `listRenewalRequestsForClinic()` in `lib/db/shares.ts`), never the whole history. |
| `/admin/links` | **Making a link.** Category pills and a search box, and one row per video this clinic may share with one button, **Create link**, in the brand colour (never red). Pressing it opens a small box on that row, "Which doctor is this link from?", with a dropdown of the people holding a seat and **nothing picked in advance** (decided by Evan on 2026-09-25, after trying one picker at the top of the page: the doctor is chosen for every link). Choosing a doctor makes one new link and turns the box into its menu: Copy link, Download QR code, Print QR code. Closing the menu does not delete the link. **There is no list of past links, on purpose** (Prompt 4 of the 2026-09-21 build plan): the app does not know which patient got which link, so the office never needs to find one again, and there is no cancel button. The pamphlet (`/admin/print/<code>`) and the QR picture (`/admin/qr/<code>`) belong to it. **Printed QR codes** (Prompt 9) live here too: a second button on each row, **Printed QR code**, asks the same doctor question and makes the permanent code for that procedure from that doctor (or shows the one already live), with Print (`/admin/qr-codes/<id>`) and Download (`/admin/qr-codes/<id>/image`); and at the bottom of the page one bounded section, **Printed QR codes**, lists the clinic's live codes with Print, Download, Replace and Retire. A printed code is the one thing an office has to find again, because it must be retirable; it is not a list of patient links. See "Printed QR codes". |
| `/admin/people` | Everyone in the clinic and the clinic's seats: "2 of 3 seats in use", Member / Member with admin on each person, Give a seat and Remove, the name patients see on each seated person's links ("Jane Smith" by default since 2026-11-16, "Dr. Jane Smith, DO" once a title is chosen) with Change, which opens the name and title editor, inviting someone (an invitation holds a seat) and revoking an invitation, the account owner's handoff, and in plain words anything that needs settling (people waiting for a seat, a plan that pays for fewer seats than are taken, no account owner). "Choose a plan first" until the clinic is open, with one exception: a list of the other people in the clinic with Remove, so an admin who has left a closed clinic can still be taken out (Remove is the one People action that works while closed; `getClinicIdForRemoval()` in `lib/clinic.ts`). Clerk's own organization panel is not on this page, or anywhere on our screens (taken off in October 2026, Prompt L): the clinic's name and logo are changed on Branding. See "Roles" and "Surgeon seats". |
| `/admin/billing` | Plan, categories, seats, and an estimate labelled as one; the plan picker and the way to Stripe's payment page for a clinic that may pay by card; the subscription it is paying for once it has one, with any change that is waiting (scheduled for the renewal, or an upgrade whose payment has not gone through); the form for changing a paid plan; and the way to Stripe's own billing page for the card, invoices and cancelling. Any office admin may change the clinic's billing, the account owner included (decided by Evan on 2026-10-05). `/admin/billing/return` is where Stripe sends the admin back to. **The only place plan or price information appears on the clinic side.** The plan on file and its estimate come from `getBillingView()` in `app/admin/billing/billing.ts`, which reads the plan through `getClinicPlan()` and quotes it with the engine in `lib/pricing.ts`; what is offered for sale comes from `getCheckoutView()` in `app/admin/billing/checkout-view.ts`. No page repeats the arithmetic. |
| `/admin/branding` | The clinic's name and its logo upload, one brand colour, a font from the short list, and office phone. Admins of open clinics only; every save is logged. |
| `/admin/reports` | **Not a section.** It only redirects to `/admin`, so an old bookmark never breaks, and it is in no navigation, menu or overview card. **Clinics get no reports or analytics at all for now** (decided by Van on 2026-10-08, replacing the "Coming later" placeholder Evan approved on 2026-10-07: numbers handed to a clinic could talk it out of renewing; revisited only if clinics ask), so no play starts or other usage numbers appear anywhere on the clinic side. Reports are for Pulse staff, on `/pulse/reports`. |

**Never put a list of past links back on the clinic side (the overview shows the newest five links the office or a surgeon made, never the ones printed codes handed out, and a few counts, and that is all; the Printed QR codes list on `/admin/links` is of codes, not of patient links), and never put plan or billing detail on the overview or on the links page.**
Every admin page draws itself inside `AdminFrame` (`app/admin/AdminFrame.tsx`): the app shell, the clinic's name, the row of section links (`AdminNav`, driven by the list and the current-page rule in `lib/admin-nav.ts`), then the page's own title.
The frame is drawn only after the page has checked that the person is an admin; a member gets `<AdminsOnly />` and no navigation at all.
An admin of a closed clinic does get the frame on every page, with the closed-clinic message in place of the content, so Billing is always one tap away.

**Billing stays reachable when the clinic is not open.**
A clinic that is PENDING, PAST_DUE, PAUSED or CANCELED cannot use the library or make new links (`clinicIsOpen()` says so), but its admin must still be able to open `/admin/billing` to see why and fix it: choose a plan, update a card, restart.
So **permission to view and repair billing is separate from permission to use the app**, and `/admin/billing` is never behind the `ClinicClosed` page: it checks `isAdmin` and deliberately not `clinicIsOpen()`, and shows the status with what it means instead.
A clinic that is managed by Pulse (`managedByPulse`) sees its plan as read-only, with no self-serve billing controls and a line saying Pulse manages it.
A member who opens it is told, in plain words, that billing is handled by the clinic's office admins.
What can be done there is in `app/admin/billing/actions.ts` and works for a closed clinic: choosing a plan and going to pay for it, and checking on a payment (see "Self-serve checkout"); and, for a clinic that has paid, Stripe's billing page and the missed payment (see "Plan changes and billing recovery").
Nothing on the page asks what kind of practice the clinic is: only Pulse staff mark a hospital, on the clinic's `/pulse` page, and a clinic that was never marked is treated as a clinic (see "Who may pay by card" under "Billing state").
Where no plan can be chosen (a hospital, a managed clinic, a clinic Pulse paused by hand, a deployment where checkout is not open, no published prices) the page says so and points at Pulse; it never shows a dead button.
Where checkout is not open (production today) or the prices cannot be read, the card is headed "Getting your clinic open" and has a **Schedule a call with Pulse 3D** button (`PULSE_CONTACT_URL`), instead of the old "Coming here" with nothing to do (audit C1, 2026-11-16).
A clinic whose payment failed but whose grace period is still running is open, and the page says the payment failed and until when.
