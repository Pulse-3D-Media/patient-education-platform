# The clinic admin area (`app/admin`)

Moved out of the root `CLAUDE.md` in November 2026, word for word except where the pull request that moved it says otherwise.
A section name in quotes ("Billing state", "Roles") is a heading in one of the files under `docs/areas/`; the index at the end of the root `CLAUDE.md` says which.

## `app/admin`, the back-office surface

**`app/admin` is the back-office surface**, and its pages have fixed jobs.
Keep this map stable; do not move a job to another page or merge two of them:

| Page | Job |
|---|---|
| `/admin` | The office manager's **overview** (never called "Inbox"): a list of what wants a look, each with its detail beside it (the approved design, screen 08). Under **New**, each with a dot: a paused link a patient has asked to have turned back on (its procedure, who it is from, when it paused and when they asked, a picture of the patient page it would bring back, and the Confirm button right there: the same `ReactivateForm` and Server Action as `/admin/reactivate/<id>`, which every check runs again; a link the plan no longer allows is marked, with See why, and no Confirm; see "How a link expires, pauses, and is turned back on"), an invitation nobody has accepted yet (with a way to it on People), a printed code that needs a look (its surgeon holds no seat, or its video is not available; with a way to it on Shared links), and, as one sentence, links that point at a video that is not available. Under **Earlier**: printed codes made in the last 30 days (at most three). The notice from Pulse sits above the list. **No counts, no list of patient links, no play starts and no other usage numbers** (the counts and the newest five links came off with the design of 2026-11-19; usage numbers came off on 2026-11-16 by the user's decision, matching Van's of 2026-10-08). Bounded: at most ten waiting links (`listRenewalRequestsForClinic()`), one count of links that do not work (`summarizeSharesForClinic()`), the clinic's live printed codes (at most a hundred, `listLiveQrCodesForClinic()`), and Clerk's list of open invitations (`listOpenInvitationsForClinic()` in `lib/seat-changes.ts`, a read only). |
| `/admin/links` | Called **Shared links**. **Making a link**, and the printed codes (the approved design, screen 09). Two equal tabs across the top (Evan, 2026-11-20), each a real link with the tab in the address, so a refresh, Back and a page without scripts behave: **Temporary patient link** (`/admin/links`, open first) and **Permanent QR code** (`/admin/links?tab=qr`; an address naming a code, `?code=<id>`, opens it too). Temporary patient link: a Procedure dropdown (the videos this clinic may share, by category) and a From dropdown (the people holding a seat, **nothing picked in advance**, decided by Evan on 2026-09-25: the doctor is chosen for every link), then **Create link** in the brand colour (never red). The new link appears with its QR code and **Copy**, **QR on screen** (the code as large as the screen allows, for a patient at the desk), **Print** (the pamphlet, `/admin/print/<code>`) and **Download QR** (`/admin/qr/<code>`). Making another link replaces it on screen; the first keeps working. **There is no list of past links, on purpose** (Prompt 4 of the 2026-09-21 build plan): the app does not know which patient got which link, so the office never needs to find one again, and there is no cancel button. **Permanent QR code** (Prompt 9): the live printed codes as a list with the chosen code's detail beside it, and **+ New code** at the top, which asks the same two questions and makes the permanent code for that procedure from that doctor (or shows the one already live), with Print (`/admin/qr-codes/<id>`) and Download (`/admin/qr-codes/<id>/image`); each live code in the list opens its detail with Print, Download, Replace and Retire (both ask first), marked "needs a look" when its surgeon holds no seat or its video is not available. The list is bounded (at most a hundred, and it says so when there are more). A printed code is the one thing an office has to find again, because it must be retirable; it is not a list of patient links. See "Printed QR codes". |
| `/admin/people` | Everyone in the clinic and the clinic's seats (the approved design, screen 10). A list on the left: the seats bar ("4 of 4"), in plain words anything that needs settling (people waiting for a seat, a plan that pays for fewer seats than are taken, no account owner, an owner whose admin was switched off), everyone in the clinic ("Owner · sends videos", "Member · waiting for a seat"), and **Waiting to join** (the open invitations); **+ Invite** at the top (an email and Member or Member with admin; an invitation holds a seat). A person's detail: where they stand with a seat ("Sends videos to patients (uses a seat)"), the name patients see on their links ("Jane Smith" by default since 2026-11-16, "Dr. Jane Smith, DO" once a title is chosen) with Change, which opens the name and title editor, the **Admin** switch (saves when flipped; the owner's stays on; turning off your own asks first, saying you cannot turn it back on yourself and another office admin would have to, Evan, 2026-11-20), Give a seat, Take a seat or Give up my seat where they apply, **Remove from clinic** (asks first), a picture of the patient page with their name for a seated person, and, for the owner looking at themself, handing the account to another admin. An invitation's detail has **Revoke invitation** (asks first). Every change goes through `lib/seat-changes.ts`. "Choose a plan first" until the clinic is open, with one exception: a list of the other people in the clinic with Remove from clinic, so an admin who has left a closed clinic can still be taken out (Remove is the one People action that works while closed; `getClinicIdForRemoval()` in `lib/clinic.ts`). Clerk's own organization panel is not on this page, or anywhere on our screens (taken off in October 2026, Prompt L): the clinic's name and logo are changed on Clinic branding. See "Roles" and "Surgeon seats". |
| `/admin/billing` | One page (the approved design, screen 12): the title with where the clinic stands and the next payment ("Active · next payment December 5") and "Test mode, no card is charged"; **Your plan** (categories, seats in use, how it is billed and the next payment, with the real amounts once there is a subscription, or the plan on file with an estimate labelled as one), with **Change plan**, which opens the form for changing a paid plan below it (the library's "Add to your plan" link opens it too); the plan picker and the way to Stripe's payment page for a clinic that may pay by card; any change that is waiting (scheduled for the renewal, or an upgrade whose payment has not gone through); **Payment method** (the card on file, read from Stripe) with **Update on Stripe** (Stripe's own billing page, for the card, every invoice and cancelling); **Billing history** (the newest six invoices, read from Stripe; see "Plan changes and billing recovery"); and Check with Stripe. Any office admin may change the clinic's billing, the account owner included (decided by Evan on 2026-10-05). `/admin/billing/return` is where Stripe sends the admin back to. **The only place plan or price information appears on the clinic side**, apart from the sign-up steps a new clinic's owner goes through before the clinic opens (`/onboarding/plan`, `/onboarding/payment`; see "Sign-up's plan and payment steps" in `docs/areas/billing.md`), and setup's "Add a seat?" confirmation (see "One more seat from setup" in `docs/areas/billing.md`). The plan on file and its estimate come from `getBillingView()` in `app/admin/billing/billing.ts`, which reads the plan through `getClinicPlan()` and quotes it with the engine in `lib/pricing.ts`; what is offered for sale comes from `getCheckoutView()` in `app/admin/billing/checkout-view.ts`; the payment method and history come from `getBillingHistory()` in `app/admin/billing/history.ts`. No page repeats the arithmetic. |
| `/admin/branding` | Called **Clinic branding**, one page (the approved design, screen 11): name and phone, the logo (its own upload button), one brand colour (swatches and a hex box), a font from the short list, and Light or Dark for the staff screens, with one Save (the name goes to Clerk first through `saveClinicNameAction`, then the rest through `saveClinicBrandingAction`; if the name is refused, the rest is not sent), and a phone beside the form showing the patient page as it will look (`PatientPhonePreview`, drawn at the patient page's own size and shrunk, so it stays an honest picture; the patient page itself is not changed here). Admins of open clinics only; every save is logged. |
| `/admin/reports` | **Not a section.** It only redirects to `/admin`, so an old bookmark never breaks, and it is in no navigation, menu or overview card. **Clinics get no reports or analytics at all for now** (decided by Van on 2026-10-08, replacing the "Coming later" placeholder Evan approved on 2026-10-07: numbers handed to a clinic could talk it out of renewing; revisited only if clinics ask), so no play starts or other usage numbers appear anywhere on the clinic side. Reports are for Pulse staff, on `/pulse/reports`. |

**Never put a list of past links back on the clinic side (the Printed codes list on `/admin/links` is of codes, not of patient links), and never put plan or billing detail on the overview or on the links page.**
Overview, Shared links and People are laid out as a list with the chosen item's detail beside it (`SplitView` in `app/admin/SplitView.tsx`): on a phone, the list alone, and a tapped item's detail alone with a way back.
Every item's detail is drawn on the server and handed to it, so choosing one asks the server nothing; the chosen item is kept in the address (`?item=`, `?person=`), which is also how other pages link to one (`/admin/people?person=invitation-<id>`, `/admin/links?code=<id>`).
Clinic branding and Billing are one column each, with the same header (`DetailParts.tsx`).
**Setup is not an admin page and moves no job:** a new clinic's four setup steps live at `/onboarding/setup` (no admin menu, as drawn), and each step calls the action that already does that job here (Clinic branding's name, logo and colour, People's invite, a link made as on Shared links); see "Where the build stands" in `docs/areas/product-scope.md`.
What the design of 2026-11-19 does not draw is still built: a form keeps what was typed when a save fails, a failure gets a plain sentence and the same button to try again, and Remove from clinic, Revoke invitation, Replace and Retire ask first.
No paragraphs of explanation on these pages (decided 2026-11-16), except a warning before something that cannot be undone, a money or legal disclosure, or an error or empty state that says what to do.
Every admin page draws itself inside `AdminFrame` (`app/admin/AdminFrame.tsx`): since Evan's notes of 2026-11-20 the same frame as the library (`ClinicShell` and `AppShell` with the side rail, `SideRail`), with the clinic's logo or name and the library's search box in the top bar, and the rail's Home, Library and Admin, with the admin pages under Admin when it opens (driven by the list and the current-page rule in `lib/admin-nav.ts`); on a phone the admin pages are also a row of pills under the top bar (`AdminPills`); then the page (its title at the top of the list on the split pages).
How the rail opens and closes is in "The side rail" in `docs/areas/library.md`.
There is no "Library" button at the foot of the rail any more: Library is one of the rail's three headings, and the person's picture is at the foot.
The frame is drawn only after the page has checked that the person is an admin; a member gets `<AdminsOnly />` and no navigation at all.
An admin of a closed clinic does get the frame on every page, with the closed-clinic message in place of the content, so Billing is always within reach (under Admin in the rail, or in the row of pills on a phone).
The rail lists the library and its categories for that admin too: those pages say for themselves that the clinic is closed, because a link shown decides nothing.

**Billing stays reachable when the clinic is not open.**
A clinic that is PENDING, PAST_DUE, PAUSED or CANCELED cannot use the library or make new links (`clinicIsOpen()` says so), but its admin must still be able to open `/admin/billing` to see why and fix it: choose a plan, update a card, restart.
So **permission to view and repair billing is separate from permission to use the app**, and `/admin/billing` is never behind the `ClinicClosed` page: it checks `isAdmin` and deliberately not `clinicIsOpen()`, and shows the status with what it means instead.
A clinic that is managed by Pulse (`managedByPulse`) sees its plan as read-only, with no self-serve billing controls and a line saying Pulse manages it.
A member who opens it is told, in plain words, that billing is handled by the clinic's office admins.
What can be done there is in `app/admin/billing/actions.ts` and works for a closed clinic: choosing a plan and going to pay for it, and checking on a payment (see "Self-serve checkout"); and, for a clinic that has paid, Stripe's billing page and the missed payment (see "Plan changes and billing recovery").
Nothing on the page asks what kind of practice the clinic is: only Pulse staff mark a hospital, on the clinic's `/pulse` page, and a clinic that was never marked is treated as a clinic (see "Who may pay by card" under "Billing state").
Where no plan can be chosen (a hospital, a managed clinic, a clinic Pulse paused by hand, a deployment where checkout is not open, no published prices) the page says so and points at Pulse; it never shows a dead button.
Where checkout is not open (production today) or the prices cannot be read, the card is headed "Getting your clinic open" and has a **Schedule a call with Pulse 3D** button (`PULSE_CONTACT_URL`), instead of the old "Coming here" with nothing to do (audit C1, 2026-11-16).
A clinic whose payment failed but whose grace period is still running is open, and the page says the payment failed and until when.
