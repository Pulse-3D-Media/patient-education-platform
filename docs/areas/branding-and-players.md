# Branding, the clinic mark on the video, the players, light and dark screens

Moved out of the root `CLAUDE.md` in November 2026, word for word except where the pull request that moved it says otherwise. A section name in quotes ("Billing state", "Roles") is a heading in one of the files under `docs/areas/`; the index at the end of the root `CLAUDE.md` says which.

**The name and logo sync.** On every signed-in page request `upsertClinicForClerkOrg()` copies the organization's name from Clerk, and its logo when Clerk has one. A logo set by Pulse staff survives when the organization has no logo of its own. A name changed on `/pulse` or on `/admin/branding` is written to the Clerk organization as well (`lib/organization.ts`), so it does not change back, and a logo uploaded on `/admin/branding` goes to the Clerk organization too, so the sync keeps it. Both are also copied onto our row at once, with a log entry, so they show on the next request. **Clerk's copy of the name goes through the rename rule** (audit Prompt O, November 2026): the name is typed into Clerk's own form, where our rules do not run, so `nameFromClerk()` in `lib/clinic-identity.ts` cleans it on the way in (a line break or other control character becomes a space, a zero-width or text-direction character is taken out, spaces are tidied, a name past 100 characters is cut), and what it keeps always passes `readClinicName()`. It cleans rather than refuses, so **a real clinic never loses its name**: when nothing usable is left, the clinic keeps the name it has (a brand-new one is called "Unnamed clinic"). Clerk's own copy is not rewritten, because writing to Clerk on a page load would slow every signed-in page; the next rename on Branding puts a clean name in both places. **A name or logo that changed in Clerk is logged** like a Branding save: the change and one entry under "Clerk" ("Changed in Clerk: name changed from ... to ...", "a logo was set", "a new logo replaced the old one") go in one transaction under the clinic's row lock (`changeClinicWithLog()`), so two visits at once log it once, and a rename or upload made on Branding, already copied with its own entry, writes nothing more. The usual visit, where nothing changed, is still one read and no write. Making a new clinic is not logged.

## Clinic branding and dependable playback (Prompt 10)

Pulse staff use a clinic's **Branding** tab at /pulse/clinics/[id]; an office admin uses /admin/branding. The clinic id for the admin save comes from its server-checked session, never the form. Members and closed clinics cannot save branding. Both forms validate the whole submission; malformed or duplicate values are refused. A failed response retains the draft so it can be retried. A repeat of a saved value adds no duplicate log entry.

The approved additions are nullable Clinic.brandColor and Clinic.brandFont, in migration 20260918184417_add_clinic_branding. One hex brand colour produces readable staff and patient shades. Choices are Inter (default), Open Sans, Source Sans, Montserrat, Nunito Sans and Merriweather. Fonts are built into the deployment and served from our own origin with display: swap; optional fonts are not preloaded. Text and playback never wait for them. The patient page retains its light background, 18px body copy (20px until the player refresh: every patient-page text size went down one small step on 2026-11-17, line heights unchanged, with a little more space between blocks) and minimum 15px text. Its authored controls are at least 48px and browser zoom stays enabled. Native video control sizes remain the browser's responsibility.

The colour, font and phone follow **last save wins, all changes logged**, through updateClinicBranding() and changeClinicWithLog(). The latter locks the row before reading, describing and writing a change. No second logo store exists. **A clinic admin uploads its logo on /admin/branding** (Prompt L): a PNG, JPG or WebP of 1 MB or less (`LOGO_MAX_BYTES`, which also keeps the upload under the 1 MiB a Server Action may receive), its kind read from its first bytes on the server (`checkLogoBytes()` in `lib/clinic-identity.ts`; the file's name and the browser's claimed type are never trusted, and an SVG is refused), sent to the clinic's own Clerk organization with Clerk's backend API (`uploadClerkOrganizationLogo()` in `lib/organization.ts`, no package, no file storage of our own), and the address Clerk then serves it from copied onto `Clinic.logoUrl` with a log entry ("a new logo was uploaded"; `recordUploadedLogo()`). **The clinic's name is changed in the same section** (`readClinicName()`: one line, 1 to 100 characters): Clerk is renamed first (`renameClerkOrganization()`), then our copy, logged as "Details changed: name changed from ... to ..." (`recordClinicName()`). If Clerk refuses or does not answer (the same time limit as every Clerk question, longer for an upload), nothing is changed and the form says so in plain words, keeping what was typed or chosen (`useKeptForm()`). Both actions (`saveClinicNameAction`, `uploadClinicLogoAction` in `app/admin/branding/actions.ts`) check `isClinicAdmin()` and take the clinic from `getCurrentClinicId()`, so only admins of open clinics may save, and the organization they write to is read from the clinic's own row, never from the form. There is no way to remove a logo on our screens yet. Pulse staff may set an approved HTTPS image address in Branding. A Clerk-uploaded logo takes priority when the next signed-in request syncs it; when Clerk has no image, the sync leaves the existing Clinic.logoUrl untouched, including a concurrent Pulse save. Clearing a Clerk image does not restore an older Pulse image: there is only one stored URL. Clerk sync remains request-driven; failed sync is retried on a later request. Patient pages read the stored copy without calling Clerk. Colour, font and phone are owned by our database, not written back to Clerk.

ClinicLogo reserves a fixed box and starts with a name/text fallback. Slow, unsupported or broken pictures do not move the player. Images load directly in the browser at low priority with no referrer; there is no unrestricted server image proxy. Pulse approves the source when entering its HTTPS address. A changed address resets the image state.

**The clinic and the doctor on the video** (`ClinicMark` in `components/ui`; first decided by Evan on 2026-10-02, after Van asked on the September 28 call for the doctor and the clinic on the video itself). It works like a watermark: it shows the clinic stands behind the video. **Since the 2026-10-15 huddle it looks and behaves the way Vimeo shows a video's owner** (Evan and Van, with a Vimeo screenshot, after trying a big see-through strip over the picture and then a slim row above it): at the top left of the picture, the clinic's logo, and beside it the doctor's name on one slim dark chip with slightly rounded corners (6px, not a full pill; Evan, 2026-11-17) ("Dr. Jane Smith, DO", `Share.senderName`, with no "Sent by"; the clinic's name for a link with no doctor recorded). **The logo is cropped to fill a round frame** (Evan, 2026-10-15, kept on 2026-11-17 after a whole-wordmark tag was tried in the player refresh: "I want the circle logo"); a square icon version of a clinic's logo works best, and the Branding form says so. No logo, or one that does not load, shows the clinic's initials on dark grey (`clinicInitials()`). A second upload (the full logo and a square icon for the circle) is wanted later; it needs a storage decision first and is not built. **The video's title is not on the picture** (the patient page writes it above the player and the library's watch page under it), except in full screen, where it comes back on a chip of its own. The chips are black at `VIDEO_CHIP_ALPHA` (0.7, `lib/branding.ts`), white words at 15px and up, readable over any frame (8.4:1 over white, checked in `lib/branding.test.ts`); each is one line, ending in "..." when too long, and the patient page writes the title and the names in full above the player. **It fades away with the controls** a moment after the video starts, and comes back when it is paused, on a tap or when the mouse moves over the player. It sits on the picture itself, not on black bars around a letterboxed one (`.picture-area` and `.picture-fit` in `app/globals.css`, which measure the box with container units; a browser without them, older than iOS 16, uses the whole box). Screen readers skip it (the page says all of it in words). On the library player it carries the signed-in person's name as patients see it on their links (`libraryStripName()`: the name chosen for them, else "Dr. First Last" from the name Clerk already sends with their membership, `CurrentClinic.defaultPatientName`), and only when they hold a seat; otherwise the clinic's name. Full clinic identity is always on the page as text too.

- **What it covers.** The top left of the picture, while the controls are showing: on a phone a strip about 40px tall across the left of the picture; it is gone while the video plays untouched.
- **A wide wordmark logo gets cropped by the circle.** The Branding form says a square icon version works best on the video.
- **It is branding, never copy protection.** It is drawn by the page, not burned into the file: screenshots and screen recordings still work, and an iPhone's own full-screen player and picture-in-picture show the video without it. Burning names into the files is not planned.

Both players play inline, and **both use our own controls, laid out the way Vimeo does it** (agreed at the 2026-10-15 huddle; the patient player used the browser's own controls until then, which looked different on every phone, and the library's custom controls were decided by Evan on 2026-09-18). They share one implementation, so they behave alike: `PlayerOverlay` (what is drawn), `usePlayerControls` (play state, time, sound, full screen, fading, keys), `player-element.ts` (what is done to the video element) and `player-rules.ts` (the pure rules), all in `components/ui`. On a screen with no mouse (a phone, a tablet): back 10 seconds, play or pause, and forward 10 seconds in the middle of the picture, each on a soft dark circle so it reads over a white frame (`VIDEO_BUTTON_ALPHA`; 44px, the middle one 52px, on a phone). With a mouse: nothing in the middle, the play button at the bottom left. Which one shows is decided by the browser (the `computer:` variant), never by its name. Along the bottom, on a soft fade only as tall as that row (`.video-scrim`, at least `VIDEO_SCRIM_TEXT_ALPHA` dark where the time sits): the time, sound, full screen and a thin progress bar (on the library's watch page, for a video with chapters, with a small gap at each chapter start and the current chapter's name after the time; the patient page passes none). The amber "Placeholder animation" chip sits top right and never fades, in both players, the patient's included (so full screen carries it too); on a narrow player (under 576px wide, a phone) it says just "Placeholder", so the clinic's mark beside it keeps room for the doctor's name. The library's player has no Close any more: it lives on the watch page, which has a back arrow above it. Everything else fades HIDE_AFTER_MS (2.6 seconds) after the last touch, mouse movement or key press while the video plays, at once when the mouse leaves the player, and comes back on pause, a tap, movement, a key or the keyboard's focus arriving on the player or one of its buttons. **The keyboard is not an exception** (Evan, 2026-11-17: "make the controls fade while it plays"; for a few days before, they stayed up while the keyboard's focus was on them). **A button holding the keyboard's focus never fades with them** (Evan, 2026-11-18: "keep outline for accessabilty"; WCAG 2.4.7): while one of the player's buttons or its progress bar has the browser's keyboard focus (`:focus-visible`), the row it sits in stays drawn, ring and all, and the other rows fade as usual (`KEEP_FOCUSED_GROUP` in `PlayerOverlay`); a key pressed on it acts at once. A tap or a click never does this. When the focus is on the player itself and the controls have faded, the first key press only brings them back and does nothing else, as the first tap on a faded picture does on a phone; the next press acts (`keyFirstPressWhileHidden()`). Tab, Shift and the other keys that only move the focus or are held still do what they always do. They never fade while the video is paused, finished, waiting for data or has failed. The fade is only drawn: every button stays in the page for a screen reader, which presses a button without a key, so its press acts at once. For someone whose phone or computer asks for less motion the controls go and come back without the fade. **A finger tap on the picture shows the controls, or hides them over a playing video; a mouse click plays or pauses; a double-click is full screen** (`pictureTapAction()`). Every control is at least 44px and labelled for a screen reader, and the keyboard works on the focused player (space or K, the arrows for ten seconds, M, F). On the patient page, before the first play the Play button (with the "for education only" box above) is still the only thing to press: one plain white circle with the triangle in the middle of the picture and no words on screen (Evan, 2026-11-17: "just the circle with the triangle"), dimmed before the tick and ready after; what to do ("Tick the box above to play", then "Tap to play") is said to a screen reader only, and the circle is 60px (72px on a wide player), small enough to sit clear of the clinic's mark on a phone's 184px-tall picture, and the controls are drawn only once the video has started. The library's watch page does the same: it opens paused, with the same plain circle (its name for a screen reader is "Play" and the title) and the clinic's mark, and the controls only after the first play. The library has no volume slider or "Start over" button any more: the middle button becomes "play again" at the end. **Full screen is our own button**, in both players: the whole player goes full screen, the clinic's mark and the controls with it, wherever the browser can do that for part of a page (Chrome, Edge, Android, Safari on a Mac and an iPad, with the older `webkit` names); an iPhone cannot, so there it hands the video to the phone's own player, which shows the video alone (accepted, not fought). The browser's own controls are never shown (`controls` is off; `controlsList="nodownload"` stays). While the patient's video waits for data (after the Play tap, or buffering) a small spinner sits in the middle of the picture in place of the middle buttons, and the Play button shows one while the video is being fetched after the tick; it turns only when the phone allows motion, is hidden from screen readers, and the first wait after Play is said once in words ("Loading the video."); the "still loading" sentence still takes over at `SLOW_AFTER_MS`. Media failures show a calm retry state, and retries retain the last playback position. The patient page records only the first actual playing event per load; retries do not count a second play start. Expired, withdrawn and failed-video states offer a tap-to-call number only when the stored phone is valid. On a computer, where a tap-to-call link does nothing useful, the same number is shown as plain words instead ("Call your clinic: (801) 555-0123"); which one shows is decided by the browser with the `computer:` variant in `app/globals.css` (a mouse or trackpad that can hover), never by reading the browser's name, so the page the server sends is the same for everyone.

**Picture-in-picture is left as it is everywhere**, and like an iPhone's own full screen it shows the video without the mark. Neither the Browser pane nor headless Edge completes a request for element full screen (both leave it pending), so full screen was not seen on this machine; it, Safari and every real phone and tablet are on the device list (`docs/player-overlay-device-checks.md`). There are no captions yet (the huddle's next step is a script file per video); nothing in the controls offers them until there are. These features are branding, never copy protection.

## Light and dark staff screens

A clinic chooses **Dark or Light** on its Branding form (the same form in both places, so also on the Branding tab of `/pulse/clinics/[id]`). It is stored in the nullable `Clinic.brandTheme` (migration `20260919212810_add_clinic_brand_theme`), a short text key like `brandFont`, never a database enum: `"light"`, or nothing for dark, so a clinic that never chose and a clinic that chose dark are the same row. `parseBrandTheme()` in `lib/branding.ts` checks it when it is saved and again when it is read; anything else reads as dark.

- **It is the clinic's setting.** Not each person's, not the computer's own dark-mode setting, and nothing is stored in the browser. A member sees their clinic's mode and cannot change it. Saving it follows every other branding rule (who may save, the clinic id from the session, the whole form refused on a bad value, the draft kept, "Branding changed: mode changed from Dark to Light." in the clinic log, nothing written when nothing changed).
- **It covers the staff screens only:** everything inside the app shell, meaning `/library` and `/admin` with their menus, the Send panel, the cancel popup and Clerk's own pieces. **Not affected, on purpose:** the patient page (always light), `/pulse` (always dark), sign-in, sign-up and onboarding, the printed pamphlet, and the inside of the library's video player, which is black in both modes.
- **How it works: named colour tokens, one attribute.** No page under `app/library`, `app/admin` or the shared staff pieces in `components/ui` holds a hex code or `white/10` for a surface, a border or text. They use the token classes (`bg-ground`, `bg-surface`, `bg-sunken`, `bg-overlay`, `bg-field`, `bg-well`, `border-line`, `border-line-strong`, `text-ink`, `text-ink-soft`, `text-ink-muted`, `hover:bg-wash`, `bg-wash-strong`, `bg-scrim`, `text-warn`, `text-problem`, `text-danger`, `shadow-panel`, `shadow-drawer`, `shadow-lift`), defined in `app/globals.css`. The dark values there are exactly the colours the screens had before tokens existed and apply by default, which is why `/pulse` and sign-in are untouched. `data-theme="light"` on the app shell's outermost element gives the same names their light values. **New staff UI uses the tokens; a literal colour there is a bug in light mode.** The exceptions are things that sit on a picture or a fill and stay as they are in both modes: the labels and chips over a library tile or a thumbnail, the white box behind a QR code, the red "Yes, cancel it" button, the amber placeholder badge, and the clinic's mark on the video (the round logo and the dark chips) with the players' controls.
- **The server writes the attribute** (`staffLook()` in `app/brand-look.ts`, through `ClinicShell` to `AppShell`), so the first paint is already right. Never set it from a script after load.
- **The accent is worked out per mode.** `staffTheme(color, mode)` lightens the clinic's colour until it stands out from near-black, or darkens it until it stands out from the darkest light surface (`STAFF_LIGHT_GROUND`, the icon rail). `text-brand-bright` is a pale shade on dark and a DARK shade on light. The shell also carries the dark-ground shades (`darkGroundVars()`), and anything marked `data-theme="dark"` inside a light clinic, which today is the video player overlay, goes back to the dark tokens and those shades; an element marked that way must also set `text-ink` itself, because an inherited text colour does not change with the attribute.
- **Clerk's pieces** get their colours from `StaffClerkProvider`: left exactly as they were in a dark clinic, given the light screens' white, ink and accent in a light one.
- **The logo sits on the banner with nothing behind it,** so a dark logo suits Light and a white or pale one suits Dark. The form says so in one sentence; no chip comes back behind the logo. The banner is 64px tall (the rail's width), the page's own colour, with one hairline under it. A logo saved with a background of its own still shows it: a white one vanishes on the white Light banner and shows as a white rectangle on the Dark one.
- **Readable by test, not by eye.** `lib/branding.test.ts` reads the light block of `app/globals.css` itself and checks every kind of text (4.5:1) and every button or text-box edge (3:1) on every light surface, plain and under a hover wash, plus the accent for all 216 grid colours in both modes. Change a light value and that test says whether something stopped being readable.

**Pilot acceptance is still required.** Neither styling nor mocked tests prove usable narration captions, real-device playback or the two-second first-frame target. See docs/branding-verification.md for actual checks and remaining sign-in, physical-device and caption acceptance. No claim of readiness is made until those checks are recorded.
