# The patient page: watch, the disclaimer record, expiry and pausing

Moved out of the root `CLAUDE.md` in November 2026, word for word except where the pull request that moved it says otherwise. A section name in quotes ("Billing state", "Roles") is a heading in one of the files under `docs/areas/`; the index at the end of the root `CLAUDE.md` says which.

## `app/watch`, the patient page

**`app/watch` is the patient's page, and it stays about one thing.** No account, no email, no app to install, no consent screen, no cookie banner before the video plays. One procedure, the clinic's name (and logo), calm plain language, large controls, and nothing else: no navigation, no related videos, no sales, no link to anywhere but play. **One box comes before the video (decided by Evan and Van at the huddle on 2026-10-08, reversing the earlier rule that the page had no second confirmation and no box to tick):** just above the video, unticked, "I understand this video is for education only. It is not medical advice, and I will ask my doctor about anything I am unsure of." (`DISCLAIMER` in `lib/education-note.ts`, which keeps the words and their version together, so changing the words means changing the version). It is a checkbox on the page, never a separate screen or a popup, and it never mentions consent. The video stays frozen until it is ticked, and the server enforces that: the page's HTML carries no playable address (a still at most, `posterForVideo()`), and ticking posts to `POST /watch/<code>/accept` (a route, so the Vercel rule covers it), which records the tick on the link and only then hands out the video (see "The disclaimer record"). Play (a plain white circle with the triangle, no words on screen) is drawn dimmed until then, and says "Tick the box above to play" to a screen reader. **Once ticked, the box folds away** (it cannot be unticked; a reload brings it back, unticked) and Play turns from dimmed to ready as soon as the video has arrived. **Ticking never starts the video: only the Play tap does** (both decided by Evan on 2026-10-10). The box is the page's one "for education only" statement: the sentence that used to sit under the video (`EDUCATION_ONLY`, Van's wording, approved by Evan on 2026-10-02) was taken off the patient page when the box arrived (option B, chosen by Evan on 2026-10-10), and now appears only on the printed pamphlet. So once the box is ticked and gone, no "for education only" words remain on the page while the video plays (accepted with option B and the box folding away). Neither wording is changed without asking. See the speed rule below. The other exception to "nothing but play": a link that has paused shows one button, Ask my clinic, and no box to type into (see "How a link expires, pauses, and is turned back on"). **And one quiet button, "Save or share this link"** (decided by the user on 2026-11-16, audit finding B11), under the video's length and deadline lines, so a patient can keep their link or pass it to someone coming with them: it opens the phone's own share sheet with the page's address and the procedure's name, or, where there is none, copies the address and says "Link copied." It sends nothing to our server, records nothing, asks for nothing, and never touches the video (`app/watch/share-link.ts`, `ShareLinkButton.tsx`). Its words, the no-JavaScript sentence and the screen reader's announcements (`player-announcement.ts`) were approved as written by Evan on 2026-11-18. It shows on a working patient link's page, and on a printed code's page only once the Play tap has made the visit's own link and the address has become `/watch/<that code>`; never on a paused, finished, expired, taken-down or not-found page. **For screen readers and the keyboard** (the same PR): each state the patient does not cause by pressing something that says so itself (the video ready, loading, not loading, the link ended, the clinic asked, a tap that did not go through) is said once through one always-present polite status region whose words change (`player-announcement.ts`); the focus moves to the next thing to read or press when the control holding it goes away; the player's controls fade while the video plays for the keyboard too (Evan, 2026-11-17: "make the controls fade while it plays"), except the row holding the keyboard's focus, which stays drawn with its ring (Evan, 2026-11-18: "keep outline for accessabilty"), and any key or the keyboard's focus arriving brings them back (see the players in `docs/areas/branding-and-players.md`); the progress bar reads as words ("1 minute 5 seconds of 4 minutes 12 seconds"); and a browser without JavaScript gets a `<noscript>` sentence with the clinic's number. **The page says when the link stops working** (decided by Evan and Van at the huddle on 2026-10-08, so a patient knows to ask for more time before it runs out; it reverses the earlier rule that nothing on the patient page mentioned a deadline): one quiet line under the video's length, the same 15px grey as that line (16px until 2026-11-17), never red and never "expire". A first-play link nobody has played yet says "Once you start watching, this link works for 10 days." with the days copied onto that link; a played first-play link and a legacy (fixed-date) link say "This link works until Friday, October 18." A printed QR code's page says what a new link would carry, from `getShareTerms()` (kept, decided by Evan on 2026-10-10). The date is the day only, with no time of day, although a link stops at the time of day of its first play, not at midnight (also decided by Evan on 2026-10-10). When the "stops working in a few minutes" note shows, the line is left out. Paused and finished links keep their own pages, with no date. The words come from `patientDeadline()` in `lib/expiry.ts`, worked out from `shareExpiryState()` with no rule of its own. The date is written in the patient's own time zone: the server draws it in Utah time and the browser rewrites it in the phone's zone as the page comes to life (`app/watch/LinkDeadline.tsx`), because the server cannot know where the patient is. The line "Watch it as many times as you like, and show it to anyone coming with you." was removed the same day (Van: nobody needs that explained).

**What `viewCount` means.** It counts play starts on the patient page: one per page load, the first time the video actually starts playing (the browser's `playing` event, not the tap, not the page opening, not a text message previewing the link, not the poster or the first bytes being fetched, and not a pause or a resume), for a published, unexpired link. It is not unique patients, not completed watches, and not evidence that anyone understood anything (see Writing copy). Any number derived from it is labelled by what it actually measures, and a link with none is "Not played yet", never "Not opened".

**How a link expires, pauses, and is turned back on.** The rule is `lib/expiry.ts`, pure, and every page and write asks it. A link made by `createShare()` is a **first-play link** (`Share.expiryPolicy` FIRST_PLAY): it stops after the platform's `unclaimedDays` (90 by default; Evan sets it to 365 on `/pulse/settings`) if nobody plays it, and the first real play (`recordSharePlay()`, called by the patient player) moves `expiresAt` to that moment plus the days copied onto the link when it was made (`Share.daysAfterFirstPlay`: the clinic's `viewDaysOverride` when Pulse staff have set one, else the platform's `viewDays`, 7 by default; Evan sets it to 10). That move happens exactly once: the claim is one UPDATE whose WHERE says "still unclaimed, still before the deadline, video still published", so two first plays at once move the deadline once, and later plays only add to the count. A play at the deadline itself or after it, on a cancelled link, or on an unpublished video writes nothing, whenever the page was opened: the server's clock decides. Every link made before this rule, and any link written without the column, is a **legacy link** (FIXED, the database default): its date was set when it was made and playing it changes nothing but the count. No backfill touched them, and none ever should. A settings edit changes links made from then on only; the numbers a link carries are the ones it was promised with, and a save that lands while a link is being made waits for it (the settings lock, above). **Both numbers have limits: a whole number of days from 1 to 365** (`MIN_LINK_DAYS` and `MAX_LINK_DAYS` in `lib/expiry.ts`, the limit the clinic override always had). The settings form and the override form refuse anything outside them, and `resolveShareTerms()` refuses again right before the numbers become dates, with a `ShareTermsError` whose message is a plain sentence and nothing written; `/admin/links` then says links cannot be made and asks for Pulse 3D instead of failing. A stored days-after-first-play outside the limits (nothing in the app writes one) never becomes a deadline either: `canClaimFirstPlay()` treats it as a fixed link. The overview's newest links say all of this in words (`shareExpiryState()`), and `/admin/links` and the library's Send panel read the same resolved numbers (`getShareTerms()`) that `createShare()` copies onto a new link, so the words and the link cannot disagree. The patient page says when the link stops working (`patientDeadline()`, see the four surfaces): the days a first play will give, or the deadline's date once there is a fixed one, and nothing once the link has run out. A play that could not be recorded is simply not counted, and a first-play link then keeps its longer unclaimed deadline, so the patient is never worse off for it.

**When those days run out, a played link is not over: it has PAUSED** (the build plan's word is "dormant"; decided by Evan and Van on 2026-09-21, reversing the old rule that there was no renewal). Nothing is ever deleted. The rule is `renewalState()` in `lib/expiry.ts`: a first-play link that has been played and has run out is **paused** while renewals are left, and **finished** otherwise; `lib/db/shares.ts`, the patient page and the admin page all ask it, so they cannot disagree.

- **The patient side.** A paused link shows a calm page with one button, Ask my clinic (`AskClinic.tsx`), and **no box to type into**: a patient would type personal details into any box that was there (rule 2). The tap sends the link's code and nothing else; `requestShareRenewal()` (public, by code, the third such function) writes one timestamp, `Share.renewalRequestedAt`, and adds one to the link's request tally, `Share.renewalRequests` (for the Pulse reports; only goes up, never cleared), **at most once per link per day** (`RENEWAL_REQUEST_GAP_MS`), in one UPDATE whose WHERE says "still paused, and not asked about within the last day", so two taps at once record one request and the clinic is told once. The page then says "Your clinic has been asked. Try this same link again in a day or so", and shows that instead of the button on any visit within the day. No deadline, no count, nothing technical.
- **The clinic side.** A request emails every office admin of the clinic, each a copy addressed to them alone so no admin sees the others' addresses (`lib/renewal-email.ts` through `lib/email.ts`, one request to Resend's batch address under an idempotency key that is a SHA-256 fingerprint of the link's row id and the request's time): the procedure, the surgeon the link is from, when it was made, how many renewals are left, and one button, Reactivate this link. Nothing about the patient, because nothing exists, and the email says so. The button opens `/admin/reactivate/<id>`: a page that needs a sign-in, is admins-only, looks the link up by its row id within the signed-in admin's own clinic (another clinic's link, a forged id, or an address with the link's code in it, is not-found), shows the link's details and where it stands, and has a Confirm button that is a POST (`reactivateLinkAction`). **No link code anywhere on this path**: the patient link's code is what plays the video, and Resend keeps every message it sends, so the email, the overview's buttons, the page's address and its form name the link by its row id (`Share.id`), which plays nothing (decided by Evan on 2026-10-15; the code addresses were dropped outright, since no renewal email had ever been sent). **Opening the email, or the page, changes nothing**: mail programs and hospital filters open links to scan them, and a forwarded email must not be able to turn anything on. Only Confirm does. The address in the email is built from a trusted origin (`pickTrustedOrigin()`), never from the Host header as it came. **At most ten of these emails per clinic per UTC day** (`RENEWAL_EMAILS_PER_DAY` in `lib/expiry.ts`, a constant on purpose; decided by Evan on 2026-10-08 in Prompt M): the first ten requests recorded at a clinic in a day each send the email; after that a request is still recorded and still listed under "Links waiting to be reactivated", and no email goes out until midnight UTC. `requestShareRenewal()` takes the clinic's renewal lock (`lockAndCountRenewalRequests()` in `lib/db/renewal-lock.ts`, a named Postgres lock per clinic, so link making and settings are never held up), counts the requests recorded there since midnight UTC, and writes its own in the same transaction, so two requests at once can never both be the tenth email; it answers `emailClinic`, and the ask route emails only when that is true. A link turned back on has its request cleared, so it leaves the count; only an office admin can do that. The waiting list stays as it was (the ten most recently asked, newest first): under a flood of requests the older ones scroll off it, which a later prompt could answer with a count or a page of its own.
- **Without email** (no `RESEND_API_KEY` and `EMAIL_FROM`, the service down, no admins, no trusted origin), the request is still recorded, and the overview lists it under **Links waiting to be reactivated** (`listRenewalRequestsForClinic()`, bounded, most recently asked first) with a button to the same page. That list is also where an admin finds a request whose email they missed. The request is written before any email is attempted, and a failed email is one word in the server log, never the code.
- **Turning it back on** is `renewShareForClinic(clinicId, shareId, actorName)`: one transaction that holds the clinic row FOR SHARE (the clinic must be open: a paused, cancelled or out-of-grace clinic is refused, and the page says why), the link's row FOR NO KEY UPDATE and its video's row FOR SHARE (`lockShareForRenewal()` in `lib/db/access.ts`) and the settings lock shared, asks the rule and the plan, and only for a **paused** link with a published video the plan still allows moves `expiresAt` to now plus the link's own `daysAfterFirstPlay`, adds one to `Share.renewalsUsed`, sets `lastRenewedAt`, clears the request (so the link is not listed as waiting again the moment it next pauses), and writes one clinic-log entry under "<name> (clinic admin)", all together. **Two admins, or a double click, renew once**: the second waits for the first and is told the link is already working. A working link, a finished one, a legacy link, one nobody played, one whose video is not published, a code that is not this clinic's, and a clinic that is not open are all refused with a plain sentence and nothing written. Plays count again from then on; the first play is history and never moves the deadline again.
- **It follows the plan** (decided by Evan on 2026-10-08, after the security audit found it did not): a paused link is NOT turned back on once its video's category is off the clinic's plan, or once the video is a placeholder and the clinic is set to finished animations only. `planBlockFor()` in `lib/access.ts` asks `decideVideoAccess()`, the rule `createShare()` asks, with the clinic and video rows locked as `createShare()` locks them, so a plan change or a placeholder mark arriving at that moment waits for the reactivation (and applies to the next one). The refusal is one sentence (`planBlockMessage()`: "This video is no longer on your plan, so this link can't be turned back on.", or the placeholder one) with nothing written. `/admin/reactivate/<id>` shows that sentence, draws no Confirm, and for a category that is for sale offers the library's "Add to your plan" link to Billing (`lib/add-to-plan.ts`); the overview's waiting list keeps such a link, marked "Not on your plan" or "Placeholder hidden for your clinic" with "See why" instead of "Turn it back on" (`RenewalRequestRow.planBlock`). The link itself is never changed, so once the category is back on the plan (or placeholders are shown again) it can be turned back on as before. A link that is still working is not affected: it runs to its own deadline, as every issued link does.
- **How many times: `AppSettings.maxRenewals`**, on `/pulse/settings`, default 3, from 0 to 10 (`MIN_RENEWALS`, `MAX_RENEWALS`; 0 means no link can be turned back on). **Unlike the day counts it is not copied onto the link: it is read at the moment it is needed, so a change applies to every link at once, links already sent included** (decided by Evan on 2026-09-25). A stored value outside the limits means zero, never unlimited. Lowering it on `/pulse/settings` asks for a yes first, in words (to 0: every paused link everywhere is finished at once), and every settings change goes in the server log under the staff member's name. When the renewals are used up and the link runs out again it is **finished**: the calm expired page, telling the patient to ask the practice for a new link, with no button.
- **What is never paused.** A **legacy link** (FIXED) behaves exactly as before: its date was final, and playing it changes nothing but the count. A first-play link **nobody ever played** that ran out is finished, not paused (decided by Evan on 2026-09-25): it was never opened, so there is nothing to give back. Every first-play link made before this shipped can pause and be renewed like a new one, for the days it was issued with (7 for most of them); no row was changed for it (decided the same day). A paused link whose video is unpublished shows the calm expired page and cannot be turned back on until the video is back, like every link to an unpublished video.
- **Email setup**, done by Evan, not by code: a Resend account, the sending domain verified there (the DNS records Resend lists, added at the domain's registrar, so the emails are not treated as spam), then `RESEND_API_KEY` and `EMAIL_FROM` in `.env` and in Vercel. Until then requests are recorded and listed, and nobody is emailed.

## The disclaimer record

The patient page's "for education only" box (decided by Evan and Van on 2026-10-08) leaves an **anonymous record on the link**, never about the person (decided by Evan the same day; schema approved, three additive columns on `Share`, migration `20261009180000_add_share_disclaimer_record`): `disclaimerFirstAcceptedAt` (set once), `disclaimerAcceptances` (one per recorded tick) and `disclaimerVersion` (the wording ticked last). **Nothing about who ticked it is stored or sent: no IP address, no user agent, no device or cookie id, no location** (rule 2), and nothing is kept in the browser either, so a reload shows the box unticked and a tick there is another record.

- **A patient link.** Ticking posts the wording's version to `POST /watch/<code>/accept` (`app/watch/[code]/accept/route.ts`). `acceptShareDisclaimer()` in `lib/db/shares.ts` is ONE UPDATE: its WHERE is the patient page's own test (the link exists, is before its deadline, its video is published) and its SET adds one tick, sets the first time only if it is empty (COALESCE) and records the version, and it hands back what playback needs. So nothing is recorded on a link that is not working, and nothing is handed out without a record. Two ticks at once are both counted and the first time is set once (`lib/db/shares.disclaimer.test.ts` forces the overlap). A tick is not a play: the deadline, the play count and the first play do not move.
- **A printed code.** See "Printed QR codes": the tick gets the video from `/q/<code>/accept`, which writes nothing, and the record is written onto the visit's own link when the Play tap makes it.
- **The words and their version** are one constant, `DISCLAIMER` in `lib/education-note.ts`. The routes record only the current version; a page that shows other words (drawn by another deployment around a release) gets a 409 and reloads.
- **Slow or failed.** The browser's side (`app/watch/accept-disclaimer.ts`) tries again by itself (three tries, ten seconds each at most); if nothing gets through, the player says so calmly under the box with Try again, never an error page. When a tick was recorded and only the answer was lost, the repeat records another, so a link can show one more tick than was made: the number is recorded ticks, never people.
- **Where staff see it.** The clinic's `/pulse` page, Links tab, "Disclaimer accepted": the first time and the count for each link. Pulse staff only; nothing about the box appears on the clinic side.
- **No way round it on a patient link.** The two other routes that also hand out a signed address, `/watch/<code>/refresh` (a fresh token before the old one runs out) and `/watch/<code>/play` (a grant for the new deadline at the first play), hand one out only on a link with a recorded tick (`disclaimerFirstAcceptedAt` set). A real player has always recorded one first, so nothing changes for a patient. That is per link, not per page load: once a tick is recorded on a link, whoever holds the link's code can renew its address, exactly as the link already is the permission to watch. **The first play needs a tick too** (audit run 2, November 2026): `recordSharePlay()` refuses the first play of a first-play link with no recorded tick (`not-accepted`), writing nothing, so a play start posted by hand cannot start a link's days before the patient has seen the box. The tick is read in the play's own read, so this adds no query. A printed code's links are written already ticked, so their first play is claimed as before; a legacy link, and a link already played, are counted as before. Tests that are about plays rather than the box tick first with `playAfterTick()` (`lib/testing/ticked-play.ts`), as a real player does.
- **What it does and does not prove.** For a video on Mux, playback without a recorded tick on that link is truly prevented: a signed address comes only from the accept route's answer, which records first, or from the two actions above, which require a recorded tick. A video still on the Webflow CDN has a permanent public address: the record shows the box was ticked on our page, but nothing stops someone fetching the file directly. On a printed code the video is handed out at the tick and the record written at Play, so a script could fetch the video from `/q/<code>/accept` without leaving a record (the printed paper is already the permission to watch). A tick is never consent, never proof of understanding, and is never described as either.
- **The library is not gated**: the surgeon's player in the room has no box.
