# Surgeon seats

Moved out of the root `CLAUDE.md` in November 2026, word for word except where the pull request that moved it says otherwise. A section name in quotes ("Billing state", "Roles") is a heading in one of the files under `docs/areas/`; the index at the end of the root `CLAUDE.md` says which.

## Surgeon seats

**The model (decided by Evan and Van on 2026-09-21, replacing the surgeon-or-staff model of PR #28):** everyone in a clinic except the account owner holds one of the seats its plan pays for (`Clinic.surgeonSeats`), and an open invitation holds one too. There is no free "staff" kind any more and no surgeon question. Every plan has at least one seat (checkout refuses zero: `readPlanSelection()`, `quote()` and `acceptPlanForCheckout()` all say so), so nobody gets the library by signing up as owner alone. The rules are `lib/seats.ts` (pure); read the top of that file first.

**Our tables are the authority on who holds a seat.** `SeatAllocation` has one row per person holding a seat; `SeatInvitation` has one row per open invitation holding one. Together they are what is compared with the plan. Membership, roles and invitations themselves live in Clerk. A person with no row is **waiting for a seat**: they can browse and play the library as before, but cannot send links until they hold one (see Roles).

- **A seat is only taken under the clinic's row lock.** `reserveSeat()` (a person) and `holdSeatForInvitation()` (an invitation) in `lib/db/seats.ts` lock the clinic, count people and holds, and only then write (`readSeatsLocked()` in `lib/db/seat-lock.ts` is the lock-then-count). Two requests for the last seat, whether people or invitations or one of each, run one after the other and the second is refused. Lowering a plan takes the same lock. One row per person per clinic makes a repeated request harmless.
- **A solo owner is seated automatically (decided by the user on 2026-11-16, audit C3).** At the moment a clinic BECOMES OPEN WITH SEATS (its first confirmed payment, a recovery, or Pulse staff opening it by hand or setting its plan), the account owner is given a seat when all of these are true: the clinic was not open with at least one seat a moment before and is now; it has an owner; nobody holds a seat and no invitation holds one; and this has never been done at this clinic before. The rule is `ownerAutoSeatDue()` in `lib/seats.ts`; the write is `seatSoloOwnerIfDue()` in `lib/db/seats.ts`, called at the end of `reconcileSubscription()` and of `changeClinicWithLog()` (every Pulse staff change to a clinic), so every path that opens a clinic gets it and no page does. It runs inside the caller's transaction, under the clinic's row lock that every seat is taken under, so two openings at once seat the owner once (`lib/db/owner-auto-seat.test.ts` forces the overlap). It writes one log entry under "seats", starting with fixed words (`OWNER_AUTO_SEAT_WORDS`); finding that entry is how "never before" is known, without a new column, so an owner who later gives the seat up is not seated again by this, however often the clinic closes and opens. Clinics already open when this shipped are not touched. In the library, an account owner without a seat is offered **Take a seat** when one is free (`takeMySeatAction()`, which is Give a seat on People for themselves, with the same checks), and any office admin without a seat is pointed at People, instead of being told to ask an office admin (`noSeatHelp()` in `lib/seats.ts`).
- **Invitations are sent from `/admin/people`, never before the clinic is paid for.** Until the clinic is open the page says "Choose a plan first" and the actions refuse (they use `getCurrentClinicId()`, which is null for a closed clinic). `inviteSomeone()` in `lib/seat-changes.ts` holds a seat first, then asks Clerk to send the invitation with the hold's id in its public metadata (`{ seatHold: id }`), then records Clerk's invitation id on the hold. If Clerk refuses, the hold is let go; if the server dies in between, the hold lets itself go after `PENDING_WINDOW_MS` (two minutes), or is linked by the next check if the invitation did go out. An admin can only have as many open invitations as there are free seats. Revoking (`revokeInvitation()`) revokes in Clerk, then frees the hold.
- **An accepted invitation becomes that person's seat.** Clerk copies the invitation's public metadata onto the new membership, so the seat check sees the hold id on the person and turns the hold into their seat in one step, under the lock: the count never changes and nobody else waiting can take it. An invitation made outside our page (the Clerk dashboard) carries no hold: the People page lists it as holding no seat, and its person arrives waiting for one. Clerk's own organization panel, whose Members tab could send one, is no longer on our screens (Prompt L).
- **A database transaction cannot make a call to Clerk part of itself, and nothing pretends it can.** No database row lock is ever held across a call to Clerk. The one lock that is, the admin-change lock above, is a named lock only other admin changes at the same clinic wait for, and each Clerk call under it has the five-second limit. Every change that writes to Clerk (invite, revoke, remove, admin on or off, making someone owner) is ordered in `lib/seat-changes.ts` so that every way it can stop halfway leaves something harmless that `checkSeats()` puts right. Giving a seat and handing over the owner touch only our database.
- **`lib/seat-changes.ts` is the only place the Clerk writes in `lib/people.ts` may be called from.** A test reads the source tree and fails if anything else uses them, or calls Clerk's membership or invitation writes directly. Every function there looks the clinic's own organization up from the clinic's row and checks with Clerk that the person is a member of **that** organization (or, for an invitation, that it is open in that organization) before anything is written, so an id sent by the browser can never reach into another clinic.
- **Nobody is removed or relabelled to make the numbers fit.** Removing someone is an admin's choice on `/admin/people` (never the owner, never yourself); it frees their seat. People in the clinic before this model (including anyone once marked "staff") are shown as waiting for a seat, and are given one only when one is free. The Clerk `kind` label those people may still carry is ignored.
- **`checkSeats(clinicId)` brings the tables back into line with Clerk** (`planSeatCheck()` decides, `applySeatCheck()` does it under the lock and counts again before each seat it gives). It turns accepted invitations into seats, records invitation ids a failed request left out, lets go the seat of someone who has left, lets go holds whose invitation Clerk says was revoked or expired (asked one by one before any hold is let go; a hold is kept whenever Clerk cannot be asked), gives free seats to people waiting (oldest member first, never the owner), and clears the owner when the owner has left. It never removes anyone, never gives more seats than the plan has, and writes nothing (and logs nothing) when there is nothing to put right. An empty member list from Clerk is treated as "could not say". There are no background jobs, so it runs when the Clerk webhook reports a membership change or a deleted organization (see "Roles"), when `/admin/people` or a clinic's `/pulse` page is opened, after a handoff, and **before anyone is told a clinic is full**.
- **Seats cannot be lowered below the number taken without saying so.** `checkSeatReduction()` is asked, under the lock, wherever seats can go down: `acceptPlanForCheckout()` and `recordAcceptedPlan()` refuse a plan with fewer seats than are taken, people and open invitations together (this is "when it is scheduled"); `setClinicPlan()` refuses too unless `allowFewerSeatsThanInUse` is set, which the `/pulse` Plan form sends only when the staff member ticked the box that says so (`npm run db:set-plan` has `--allow-fewer-seats`). When a plan **takes effect** short of what is taken (seats can be taken between accepting a plan and paying for it), it is applied, because it was paid for, and `reconcileSubscription()` says so in the log. **A change to a paid plan asks `checkSeatReduction()` both when it is accepted (`acceptPlanForChange()`, which refuses) and again when it takes effect** (`reconcileSubscription()`, which applies what was paid for and says so in the log if it lands short). **In between, a scheduled reduction is itself the limit:** from the moment a plan with fewer seats is scheduled for the renewal, nobody can be given a seat or an invitation past that lower number (`scheduledReductionBlocks()` in `lib/seats.ts`, read under the lock by `readSeatsLocked()`), and the admin is told to cancel the scheduled change on Billing first. So a scheduled reduction cannot land below what is taken unless a row was written around the app.
- **An over-the-plan clinic is shown, never quietly fixed.** Nobody is removed, no seat is taken away and no charge is changed. The gap is said in plain words on `/admin/people`, `/admin/billing` and the clinic's `/pulse` page, the log entry says by how much, and `hasFreeSeat()` refuses every new seat and invitation until someone is removed, an invitation revoked, or the seats raised.
- **Seat and owner changes are in the clinic log**, written in the same transaction as the change where the change is ours: a seat given or let go, an invitation sent or revoked, a removal (under "<name> (clinic admin)"), a name for patients (under "<name> (clinic admin)", or "<name> (surgeon)" when a surgeon set their own), a handoff, an owner set by Pulse staff, and anything `checkSeats()` put right (under "seats"). Admin on or off is logged right after Clerk confirms it. A hold whose invitation was never made is not written up. Nothing about the person is stored but a Clerk id and, when one has been chosen, the name patients see on their links (`SeatAllocation.displayName`, staff data): other names appear only in the log's sentences, and invited email addresses live only in Clerk.
- **Who a link is from.** Every new link records its surgeon (`Share.senderUserId`) and the name patients see (`Share.senderName`), copied when it is made, so a later name change or the surgeon leaving never changes a link already sent. The name is the one chosen for them, else their first and last name from Clerk as they are ("Jane Smith"), else none, in which case the link names only the clinic; their email is never used. **The default is the bare name (decided by the user on 2026-11-16, audit C5), replacing "Dr. First Last":** not everyone holding a seat is a doctor. A name typed in Clerk that already starts with "Dr" stays as typed. Nothing stored was rewritten: names already chosen (`SeatAllocation.displayName`), names already copied onto links (`Share.senderName`) and printed QR codes' recorded fallback names (`QrCode.senderFallbackName`) keep the words they had.
- **How the name is chosen (decided by Evan on 2026-10-02).** A name box that holds only the name, and an optional title from a list (labelled "Title (optional)", with a line saying what each does): MD, DO and DPM put "Dr." in front ("Dr. Jane Smith, DO"); PA-C and NP do not ("Jane Smith, PA-C"); **Staff**, for office staff, goes after the name with no "Dr." ("Jane Smith, Staff"; added by the user's decision of 2026-11-16, shown rather than left off so that a saved Staff reads back as Staff and not as None); Other takes a short typed credential (letters and hyphens, up to 12) shown after the name with no "Dr." ("Jane Smith, LAc"); None is just the name. Nobody is given a credential in advance: someone nobody has chosen for keeps the bare-name default, and the editor opens on None for them, because that is what patients see; "Choose..." is offered only for an old "Dr." name with no title after it. The name box refuses a comma and a leading "Dr", so the "Dr." and the credential come only from the list. The finished words are stored in `SeatAllocation.displayName` (no new column): `parseNameChoice()` in `lib/sender-name.ts` checks a choice and builds the words, and `readSenderName()` reads stored words back into the choice so the editor opens on what was saved. Both are pure and the browser and the server use the same ones; the server always builds the words itself from the name and credential sent, never takes finished words from the browser. There is no "go back to the default" any more: once chosen, a name is changed, not cleared. One editor (`SenderNameForm` in `components/ui`) is used in two places. **An office admin** sets anyone's who holds a seat on `/admin/people` (`setPatientName()` in `lib/seat-changes.ts`). **A person holding a seat** sets their own from the library's Send panel, which shows "Sent as Dr. Jane Smith, DO" with Change (`setOwnPatientName()`, through `setMyPatientNameAction` in `app/library/[category]/actions.ts`): what the browser sends has no "whose name", the person is the signed-in user, so a surgeon can never change anyone else's, and someone with no seat is refused. Both are checked with Clerk, written under the clinic's lock and logged in the same transaction. The link already in the Send panel keeps the name it was made with; after a change the panel says so and offers to make the link again with the new name (the first link is left as it is, like every unsent link).
- **Staff see the sender labelled as the surgeon**, so nobody mistakes the name for a patient's (Van did, on 2026-09-28): "Surgeon:" in the renewal email, on `/admin/reactivate/<id>`, in the overview's waiting list, and on a new link's menu on `/admin/links`. The overview's newest links do not show a sender. `lib/senders.ts` lists who can be picked and checks a pick with Clerk (`resolveSender()`); `createShare()` then reads the seat with a share lock (`lockSenderSeat()` in `lib/db/access.ts`) in the transaction that writes the link and throws `SenderRefusedError`, writing nothing, when that person holds no seat at this clinic right now. The patient page and the pamphlet name the doctor on one line and the clinic on the line under it ("Dr. Jane Smith, DO", then "<clinic>"), with no "Sent by" (decided by Evan on 2026-10-05: two lines, because one line with commas left the reader to guess where the name ended); a link made before this (or by the tests and seed scripts, which pass no sender) says "From <clinic>" as before (`senderLines()`).
- **What this does not do.** A removal, a revocation or an owner leaving in Clerk's own panel is noticed within seconds when the Clerk webhook delivers its notification (see "Roles"), and otherwise the next time someone opens the People page or the clinic's `/pulse` page; the webhook finds a change, it never prevents one, and a preview cannot receive it. And that the metadata really is copied from an invitation to the membership is Clerk's documented behaviour, proved here only with the stand-in: the walkthrough with a real Clerk test organization is the check.
